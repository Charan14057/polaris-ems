## 3. Provenance Hierarchy Alignment

Every metric displayed on the Spatial Twin Canvas and inside the Equipment Inspector is tied directly to one of the six locked provenance tiers:

1. **REAL**: Actual empirical baseline data recorded during prior polar station expeditions (weather, historical hourly loads).
2. **CONFIGURED**: Declared nameplate specifications in authoritative station profiles (e.g., 400V 3-phase, 120 kWh BESS, generator capacities).
3. **ASSUMED**: Engineering parameters where empirical polar measurements are unavailable (e.g., default 0.85 inductive power factor, ambient heat transfer coefficients).
4. **SYNTHETIC**: Formally generated synthetic disturbances used in Phase 5 stress testing (e.g., synthetic storm profiles).
5. **FORECAST**: Forward predictive weather and solar irradiance estimates generated by Phase 3 ML models.
6. **SIMULATED**: Physics-conserved trajectory states calculated by the authoritative Phase 4 TwinEngine forward step.
